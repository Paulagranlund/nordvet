    How to read this

    • No-show rate = no-shows ÷ (completed + no-shows). Cancellations are excluded (nobody was expected), and so are appointments with no outcome yet.
    • Lost vet-hours = booked minutes of no-show appointments. The data has no prices, so money = vet-hours × your own value per vet-hour.
    • Cancelled after start: cancelled after the appointment time. Shown separately, not counted as no-shows.
    • Deleted appointments are excluded. Days are clinic local time.
    • Flags: ▲Above normal ≥ 2 no-shows and ≥ 8 points above the clinic's 28-day normal. !Persistently high 4-week rate ≥ 1.5× chain average. ↗Getting worse 4-week rate ≥ 5 points above the 4 weeks before.
